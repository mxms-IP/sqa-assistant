# BUG-003.md

**ID:** BUG-003  
**Title:** Chatbot icon overlaps the "Buy Now" button on mobile screens smaller than 390px  
**Severity:** High  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Open  
**Reported by:** Riya Menon  
**Build:** v0.9.2  
**Environment:** iPhone SE (3rd gen), iOS 17, Safari Mobile — screen width 375px

---

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Open Safari on an iPhone SE (3rd gen).
2. Navigate to the Samsung Galaxy S22 product page.
3. Scroll to the bottom action bar containing "Buy Now" and "Add to Cart".

**Expected result:**  
Per Technical Requirement 1, the chatbot icon location should be intuitive and not obstruct primary purchase actions.

**Actual result:**  
The chatbot icon (fixed-position, bottom-right) overlaps the "Buy Now" button. Tapping the "Buy Now" area activates the chatbot instead of initiating purchase. Reproduces 100% on 375px screen width.

**Related PRD requirement:** Technical Requirement 1  
**Related test case:** TC-002

**Notes:** Does not reproduce on Samsung Galaxy S22 (360px width) — only confirmed on iPhone SE. Engineering to investigate fixed-position z-index conflict with the native bottom action bar.
